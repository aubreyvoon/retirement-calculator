Figures behind it

  • EPF: employee 11%; employer 13% on wages up to RM5,000 a month, 12% above. Malaysia.gov.my
  • The full EPF contribution is counted. Since May 2024 it is split 75% Akaun Persaraan, 15% Sejahtera, 10% Fleksibel, and withdrawals from Sejahtera or Fleksibel before retirement will lower the result. RinggitPlus
  • EPF dividend 6.15% for 2025 and 6.3% for 2024; 5.5% is used as a long-run assumption. RinggitPlus
  • ASB paid 5.75 sen a unit for FY2025. RinggitPlus
  • 12-month fixed deposits pay about 2.05% to 2.80% a year. RinggitPlus
  • Bank Negara expects inflation of 1.5% to 2.5% in 2026; October 2026 was 1.9%. The Star, Malay Mail
  • Belanjawanku 2024/25: about RM2,690 a month for a single senior, RM3,390 for a couple. The Star
  • Unit trust, PRS and stock returns are assumptions, not published figures.
  • Not included: EPF after age 60, tax, medical costs rising faster than inflation, bonuses, one-off needs such as a home loan, education or Hajj, market falls, and early EPF withdrawals.
